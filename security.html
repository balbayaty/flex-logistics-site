1. Governance

Flex operates an information-security management programme aligned to ISO 27001 controls and SOC 2 Type II equivalent. Annual third-party review covers infrastructure, application and operational controls.

2. Encryption

  • In transit: TLS 1.3 on every customer endpoint. Older protocols disabled.
  • At rest: AES-256 for all customer data and backups.
  • Key management: Hardware-backed (HSM/KMS) with quarterly key rotation.

3. Data residency

Primary customer data is hosted inside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Disaster-recovery replicas reside in the UAE region. Cross-border transfers comply with PDPL Article 29.

4. Access control

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with least-privilege defaults.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) mandatory for all employees and admin accounts.
  • Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML 2.0 / OIDC for enterprise customers.
  • Audit logs retained for 7+ years.

5. Application security

  • Static (SAST) and dynamic (DAST) scanning in CI.
  • Dependency scanning for known CVEs; automated patch PRs.
  • Secure-by-default headers: HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Permissions-Policy, Referrer-Policy.
  • Annual third-party penetration test; report summary on request.

6. Operational security

  • Background checks for personnel handling regulated cargo.
  • Segregated duties for hazmat, customs and finance workflows.
  • 24/7 SOC monitoring of perimeter and runtime telemetry.
  • Incident-response runbooks tested twice per year.

7. Disclosure policy

We welcome responsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities. To report:

  • Email security@flexlogistics.com (PGP key on request).
  • Or follow the machine-readable contract at /.well-known/security.txt.

We commit to: acknowledge receipt within 2 business days; provide a triage update within 7 days; remediate critical issues within 30 days; coordinate disclosure timing with you. We do not pursue legal action against good-faith researchers who follow this policy.

Out of scope

  • Denial-of-service testing without prior approval.
  • Physical or social-engineering attacks against Flex personnel or facilities.
  • Third-party services not under Flex's direct control (e.g. fonts.googleapis.com, cdn.tailwindcss.com).
