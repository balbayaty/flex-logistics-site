Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 April 2026 · Version 1.0

Flex Logistics ("Flex", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal data in accordance with the Saudi Arabia Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable, and applicable data-protection regulations in every MENA jurisdiction in which we operate.

1. Who we are

Flex Logistics is a third-party logistics provider headquartered at Support Services Area, Dammam 34341, Saudi Arabia. For data-protection inquiries, contact dpo@flexlogistics.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Customer business data: company name, registered address, VAT/CR numbers, billing details, ZATCA e-invoice records.
  • Contact data: names, emails, phone numbers and roles of authorised contacts.
  • Operational data: shipment manifests, SKU details, SDS/MSDS documents, customs filings, IoT sensor readings (temperature, location).
  • Website data: IP address, user-agent, pages viewed, timestamps, cookie preferences (see §6).

3. Why we process data (legal basis)

  • Contract performance: delivering logistics services you have engaged us for.
  • Legal obligation: customs filings, ZATCA e-invoicing, SFDA reporting, Civil Defense recordkeeping.
  • Legitimate interest: fraud prevention, network security, analytics to improve services.
  • Consent: marketing emails and non-essential cookies (you may opt out at any time).

4. Data sharing

We share data only with: (a) sub-processors strictly bound by data-processing agreements (cloud hosting, payment, e-signature); (b) regulators as required by law (Civil Defense, SFDA, ZATCA, Customs); (c) carrier partners executing your shipments. We never sell personal data.

5. Data retention

We retain operational and customs data for the legally required minimum (typically 7 years for ZATCA, 10 years for Civil Defense hazmat records). Marketing data is retained until you withdraw consent.

6. Cookies

Our public website uses only essential cookies (session, language preference) by default. Optional analytics cookies (e.g. privacy-respecting Plausible) load only after consent. Tracking cookies for ad networks are not used.

7. Your rights

  • Access, rectify or erase your personal data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability (machine-readable export)
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) or your local supervisory authority

Submit requests to dpo@flexlogistics.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. International data transfers

Customer operational data is hosted primarily in Saudi Arabia (data residency). Disaster-recovery replicas reside in the UAE. Cross-border transfers comply with PDPL Article 29 and, where applicable, GDPR Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

9. Security

See our Security page for full controls (TLS 1.3, AES-256 at rest, RBAC, annual penetration testing, vulnerability disclosure programme).

10. Changes

We post material changes here with at least 30 days' notice. Minor edits are versioned in the footer. The current version is 1.0 (28 April 2026).
