# FLEX / Continuity in Motion — corporate concept
**Status:** Unpublished corporate design study hosted at a separate Vercel URL. Not scsflex.com.

## Design objective
Replace speculative sci-fi with an editorial industrial/logistics visual language and the commercial reality of FLEX Logistics Saudi Arabia. The strategic narrative is operational continuity in uncertain, shifting trade environments without naming geopolitical conflicts or making promises about specific restrictions.

## Actual implementation
- Hero: 'The route may change. The commitment doesn't.'
- Warehousing + industrial/chemical storage section with commodity-led screening
- FTL/LTL, domestic Saudi trucking and cross-border freight; customs coordination and freight forwarding
- Value-added services, integrated 3PL/4PL, industrial facility solutions
- Nine selectable corridors: UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman, Jordan, Iraq, Egypt, Saudi domestic
- Regional SVG map based on Natural Earth geographical outlines; route connections are illustrations, not navigation recommendations
- Arabic/English toggle with RTL; mobile navigation; service details; enquiry brief that can be copied/emailed
- BlueDXP/Hazalyze section presented as an evolving capability, not an operational government-authorized decision engine

## Accuracy and content policy
Historic FLEX data supports the general service footprint; do not infer licence scope, active capacity, or service-level guarantees from design text. Facility counts, insurance, certifications and route restrictions require documentary confirmation.
No live TMS/WMS/customs/border APIs are connected. Photos sourced from Unsplash are explicitly illustrative and must not be described as FLEX-owned assets.
No personal data is sent to any FLEX server by this concept. The enquiry generator creates user-controlled local text and a mailto action.
