Discuss inbound receiving, pallet storage, batch/lot tracking, order preparation, inventory reporting and outbound distribution as part of your 3PL operating design. Detailed service levels and integrations are agreed in the contract.

Dammam distribution

Use Dammam and Eastern Province as the starting point for domestic distribution or Saudi/GCC cross-border movements. Cross-border transport, brokerage and documentation arrangements can be scoped alongside storage.

Information to include in your enquiry

Origin and destination; cargo description; quantity, weight and dimensions; delivery or storage dates; documentation requirements; and any temperature, safety or handling conditions. For chemicals or dangerous goods, include the latest safety data sheet, UN number and hazard class if applicable.

Service availability, documentation requirements and cargo acceptance are assessed for each shipment. This page is a service overview, not a guarantee of regulatory approval, transport time or warehouse acceptance.

Questions buyers ask

Can FLEX store dangerous goods?

Please provide the relevant SDS and classification. Acceptance requires a facility- and commodity-specific safety and licence review; we do not promise acceptance for all DG classes or all products.

Can you handle drum, IBC and palletized chemicals?

Describe the packaging, weights and handling method in the enquiry. Suitability depends on the specific warehouse, available equipment and risk assessment.

Can I combine storage with transport?

Yes, request an integrated scope covering receiving, storage, order fulfilment and road transport, subject to commercial and operational confirmation.
